## First AI-made 3D hero (verified, renders in `assets-src/characters/`)

- Hunyuan3D 2.1's free online demo makes **shape-only** meshes anonymously (~30 s, 384 octree); the textured
  `generation_all` asks for 270 s of GPU, over the anonymous limit, and ZeroGPU quota runs out after ~3 tries a day.
- **Feed it a cut-out on black, never the grey studio image or a transparent PNG:** with the demo's own background
  removal the female came back as a 2 × 0.66 × 2 slab, and a transparent PNG added a ground slab. The clean male mesh
  came from TRELLIS.2's preprocessed (black background) image.
- Colour comes from the concept: `tools/bake.py` keeps the largest piece, simplifies to 15k faces, scales to 1.72 m,
  and paints a 2048 atlas (front = the concept, eroded 6 px to drop the grey fringe; back = a softened copy with the
  head in hair colour). The front matches the concept closely; sides streak and the back is a guess. No rig yet.
- Tools and the Godot render scene live in `assets-src/characters/{tools,render}` (git-ignored, inside the repo).
  The female hero waits for the demo's quota (about 23 h) or a local install.

## Local FLUX concepts (verified on Bram)

- mflux 0.24 takes a local model as `--model <path> --base-model schnell` (`--path` is gone) and names multi-seed
  outputs `<id>_seed_<n>.png`. Three 768×1024 images take ~4.5 min on the M4 and peak at 16 GB, so FLUX and the
  local Hunyuan3D never run at the same time.
- FLUX follows the start of the prompt most: put the character first and the style block after it. "Empty open
  hands" made it turn the palms up; "arms hanging at the sides, hands relaxed and empty" fixed it. Adding "no text,
  no watermark" stopped a fake signature. Bram's first try ignored "stout" and "balding" until they led the line.
- FLUX backdrops get lighter toward a floor with a soft shadow at ~86% height: `cutout.py` now judges the
  background per row and drops unsaturated grey pixels in the bottom 20%.
- FLUX drew "young" adults (Mira, June, Celeste) as children; the prompt sheet now starts each with "An adult woman
  in her twenties". The cut-out kept grey between the legs because that gap opens onto the floor shadow: floor
  pixels now count as background before the search (`cutout.py`). `tools/batch3d.sh` runs `STAGE=cut` (check
  `cutchecks.jpg`) then `STAGE=shape` (~22 min a character, never alongside FLUX).
