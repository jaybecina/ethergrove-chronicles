# 0023 — Outward-style folk soundtrack replaces the church-like orchestral and lo-fi style

- **Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Evidence:**
  `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-outward-style-soundtrack-design.md`

**Decision.** Music and sound effects move to small-ensemble folk fantasy in the spirit of _Outward_ (ideas and
structure only, no melody or arrangement copied): strumstick, folk harp, fiddle, recorder, psaltery, cello drones
and hand drums, folk modes, rests, and a day / night / fight set per place. The Rootsong, Grandpa's theme and the
Gloam keep their exact intervals. The synths `pad`, `choir`, `ePiano`, `arp`, VSCO `glock` bells, the lo-fi kit and
the Organ are retired and tests forbid them. Godot gets its first music and sound playback. Supersedes the style map
(§2.3) and lo-fi rules (§3.3) of the audio spec and part of [[decisions/0012-ethergrove-audio-direction]].

**Why.** The owner heard the old soundtrack as "like a church". Cause (**verified**): a synth `choir` in the title
and night tracks, `pad` under nearly every track, glockenspiel bells, and harp or glock chimes in 8 sound effects.

**Samples.** VCSL (`github.com/sgossner/VCSL`, CC0-1.0, **verified** via the GitHub API) beside VSCO 2 CE. No CC0
guitar with a verifiable licence was found, so the strumstick and folk harp fill that role.

**Rejected.** Quoting or re-creating any Outward melody (copyright); a Settings switch between old and new music
(double the download); keeping the lo-fi tracks (they read as modern, not fantasy).
