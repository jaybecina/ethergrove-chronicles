# 0004 — Original game, CC0 assets only

- **Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Evidence:** `CREDITS.md`, `README.md` intro

## Decision

Ethergrove Chronicles (formerly Hearthvale) is inspired by the farm-sim genre but copies nothing from Harvest Moon or other games: no
names, characters, art, music or text. Third-party assets are CC0 (KayKit, Quaternius, Kenney), and each
pack's license is verified before download.

## Consequences

- Requests to copy existing game music or art are declined. Offer original or CC0 alternatives instead.
- New assets need a `CREDITS.md` entry.
